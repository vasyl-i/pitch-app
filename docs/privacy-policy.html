Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 17, 2026

PitchGym ("the App") is a singing practice application developed by Anya Bedrytska ("we", "us", "our"). This policy describes what data we collect, why, and how we handle it.

Your voice stays on your device. Raw microphone audio is processed locally in real time and is never uploaded, stored on our servers, or shared with anyone.

1. Data we collect

Account information. When you create an account we store your email address, display name, and avatar URL. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive the name and email associated with that account.

Vocal profile. The App measures your vocal range (lowest and highest notes) on-device. The resulting note values and a confidence score are synced to our servers so your profile is available across devices. No audio is uploaded.

Practice data. After each practice session we store performance metrics: score, pitch accuracy, stability, rhythm, duration, and per-note statistics. This data is used to track your progress and personalise future lessons.

Learning preferences. Your goals, experience level, preferred genres, daily practice time, reminder settings, and coach style are stored to tailor lessons.

Device push tokens. If you enable practice reminders, we store a device identifier (FCM or APNs token) to deliver push notifications. Tokens are deleted when you sign out or delete your account.

Uploaded instrumentals. Audio files you import for sing-along practice are stored locally on your device. We analyse them on-device to detect key and chord progressions. The audio files are not uploaded to our servers.

Subscription information. If you purchase a premium subscription, payment is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We receive a transaction confirmation and subscription status but never see your payment card details.

2. Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the App is used in aggregate: which features are popular, where users encounter issues, and general usage patterns. Firebase may collect:

  • Device type, operating system version, and app version
  • Crash reports and performance diagnostics
  • Anonymous usage events (screens viewed, features used)
  • Country-level location (derived from IP address, not precise GPS)

Firebase analytics data is governed by Google's privacy policy. We do not link analytics data to your account or audio.

3. How we use your data

  • Provide and personalise your practice experience
  • Sync progress across your devices
  • Send practice reminders you have opted into
  • Improve the App through aggregate analytics
  • Process and manage your subscription

4. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Supabase (database hosting) — stores account, profile, and practice data
  • Google Firebase (analytics and push notifications) — receives anonymous usage events and device tokens
  • Apple (payments) — processes subscriptions via the App Store

We may disclose data if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

5. Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account (available in Account settings), all personal data is permanently removed from our servers within 30 days. Local data on your device is cleared immediately upon account deletion.

6. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent for optional processing (e.g. push notifications)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at legal@anyabedrytska.com.

7. Children's privacy

PitchGym is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Account sessions are secured with encrypted tokens stored in the device's secure keychain. All data in transit uses TLS encryption. Database access is restricted by row-level security policies ensuring users can only access their own data.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the App or by email. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
