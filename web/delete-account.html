PitchGym account deletion

What gets deleted

When you delete your PitchGym account, the following data is permanently removed from our servers within 30 days:

  • Your account information (email, display name, avatar)
  • Vocal profile and range data
  • All practice session history, scores, and statistics
  • Learning preferences, goals, and progress
  • Push notification tokens

Audio files you imported for practice are stored only on your device and are never on our servers. Uninstalling the app removes them.

What is not affected

  • Subscriptions. Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription. You must cancel it through your device's subscription settings (App Store or Google Play) before or after deleting your account to avoid continued charges.
  • Anonymous analytics. Aggregated, non-identifiable analytics data may be retained as it cannot be linked back to your account.

This action is permanent and cannot be undone. Once your account is deleted, your data cannot be recovered. Make sure to cancel any active subscriptions separately.

How to delete your account

The fastest way to delete your account is directly in the app:

  • Open PitchGym
  • Go to Profile tab
  • Scroll down and tap Delete account
  • Confirm the deletion

If you no longer have access to the app, you can request deletion below by entering the email address associated with your account.

Your email app should open with a prepared request. Send it, and we will permanently remove your account data within 30 days and confirm by email. If no email app opened, write to legal@anyabedrytska.com from the address linked to your account.

Contact

If you have questions about account deletion or your data, contact us at legal@anyabedrytska.com.
