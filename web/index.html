Frequently asked questions

Is PitchGym free?

PitchGym is free to download and includes free daily practice. Premium adds personalized feedback, adaptive daily lessons, weak spot training and advanced exercises, with a 7-day free trial.

Does PitchGym record or upload my voice?

No. Your voice is analysed on your device in real time. Raw microphone audio is never uploaded, stored on our servers or shared with anyone.

Do I need to know music theory?

No. PitchGym is made for beginner and intermediate singers. Exercises start simple and adapt to your vocal range and level as you improve.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose PitchGym and tap Cancel subscription. You keep premium until the end of the paid period.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile and choose Delete account. You can also follow these steps.
